What are the symptoms of dengue fever?
Dengue fever symptoms include high fever (104°F), severe headache, pain behind the eyes, joint and muscle pain, rash, and mild bleeding. Symptoms appear 4–10 days after a mosquito bite. Please consult a doctor if you experience these. 🏥
How can I prevent it?
To prevent dengue: use mosquito repellent, wear full-sleeve clothing, eliminate standing water around your home, and use bed nets. Vaccination is available in some regions. Stay safe! 💙
